PRO-TIP NOTICE: How to Accurately Trace & Confirm Who is Who
When searching by name, public databases often index multiple people with the same name. Always cross-reference additional intelligence (approximate age/birth year, previous city/state addresses, and listed relatives) to confirm the exact person you are tracing!

🎯 CRITICAL NOTICE: The Technique to Successfully Trace & Verify Who is Who

Public records searches often yield multiple results for common names. Simply finding a name is not enough — you need extra verification markers to lock in the true identity. Always use the background intelligence cards to cross-verify:

🎂 1. Approximate Age / DOB
Match birth month/year and calculated age against your lead profile.
📍 2. Address History & Timeline
Trace move-in timelines and past cities/states where the subject lived.
👥 3. Known Relatives & Family
Cross-check listed family members with ages to confirm family units.
💼 4. Associates & Phone Carriers
Examine co-residents, business associates, and wireless line carriers.

1. The 4 Search Modes

LeadsOSINT supports 4 dedicated intelligence search engines. Select the tab that matches your query data:

  • 👤 Name & Location: Enter full name and city/state or ZIP code (e.g. Michael Brown, Atlanta GA). Full 10/10 verified public records are parsed with address timelines, phones, and email linkages.
  • 📞 Phone Number: Reverse lookup by 10-digit US phone (e.g. 305-555-0199). Identifies registered owners, carrier types, and resident history.
  • ✉️ Email Address: Reverse email intelligence (e.g. david.miller@gmail.com). Important Notice: Unlike Name/Address searches which pull full historical dossiers, Reverse Email search specifically identifies and outlines the person or couple of people associated with or possibly owning the searched email address, along with their linked residential locations and contact records.
  • 📍 Street Address: Property and resident lookup (e.g. 1515 Q St NW, Washington DC). Reveals all current and past occupants.

2. Batch Processing & CSV Upload (Up to 500 Leads)

You can paste up to 500 leads at a time, or upload a CSV file with automatic column detection (First Name, Last Name, City, State, ZIP, Phone, Email). The system runs with 8-way parallel multithreading for rapid batch intelligence.

3. Verified Public Records & 1-Click Copy

Every result is captured with an authentic full-resolution public records screenshot and detailed narrative story. Click 📋 Copy Report Text to copy the entire public background report into your clipboard in 1 click.

4. Re-do Searches & Credit Usage

If you need to re-run a lead or refine search parameters, use the 🔄 Re-do button. Note: Each re-do search executes a live intelligence lookup and counts as 1 lead credit from your allocated plan quota.

5. Sharing Dossiers & Resetting Links

Share your findings using the 🔗 Share Report or Share Result buttons. Shared links are completely read-only (all edit, delete, and re-do buttons are securely removed for external viewers).

Security Feature: If you ever wish to stop sharing access, click 🔄 Reset / Revoke Link inside the share modal. This immediately revokes the old link (returning 404 to previous viewers) and generates a fresh private link.

6. Priority Support & Messaging

Need extra quota or have questions? Reach out directly via the in-app 💬 Messages system or click 🎧 Support for priority WhatsApp contact.
